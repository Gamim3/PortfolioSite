EMI // PROFILE

I am a 3D Artist passionate about bridging the gap between 2D illustration and 3D depth. My workflow involves Blender, Maya, and Substance Painter to create NPR (Non-Photorealistic) assets that feel alive.

Inspired by the vibrant UIs of Atlus games and the soft aesthetics of slice-of-life anime, I strive to make work that is both technically proficient and emotionally resonant.
